# EDA Findings Summary

## The story in three sentences
Revenue grew 44% in 2025 but contribution profit grew only 29%, because margin slipped from 29.6% to 26.4%. About three-quarters of the slip is product cost rising relative to list prices, concentrated in Electronics, Footwear and Camping. Deeper consumer discounting, unrecovered shipping to far markets, more Marketplace sales and faster-growing marketing spend added to the pressure, but each is smaller.

## Recommendations the evidence supports (priority order)
1. Fix cost-versus-price in Electronics and Footwear first (largest effect): review list prices and supplier terms; protect wholesale electronics from the fixed 20% discount.
2. Stop deep discounts above 20-30%, especially on Marketplace deals; they earn almost nothing and are associated with more returns.
3. Raise free-shipping thresholds or add shipping fees for UAE, South Africa and Ghana. You may consider Australia too.
4. Reduce fit-related returns with size guides and fit information for Footwear and Apparel.
5. Reassess Marketplace and paid-media budgets: lowest-value customers and rising cost per sales dollar.

## What you can and cannot claim
- CAN say: where margin fell, by how much, in which categories, channels and markets.
- CAN say: cost rose relative to list price in specific categories.
- CANNOT say: that discounts cause returns, or that supplier price increases caused the cost rise, or that price rises would not lose customers.
- Q4 2025 margin looks better partly because recent orders have not had time to be returned.
- Imputed shipping costs and marketing months are approximations.
